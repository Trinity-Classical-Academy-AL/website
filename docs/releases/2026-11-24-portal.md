# Release: hiring, next steps, roles, repeating events, forms and finance (2026-11-24)

On top of production (`main` at 38672cc). All three Netlify sites deploy from `main` and share one Neon database. Includes PR #66 (tour emails and People), which it supersedes.

## What ships

- **Production fixes (from #66):** a family's requested tour time is a private "Requested tour" (not offered to other families); one email per action (no duplicate staff alert to someone who is also the family); tour email subjects name the family.
- **People** replaces Staff & access: one row per person, a sheet to turn roles on and off, and merging two accounts that are one person.
- **Roles:** Staff is shown as **Teacher**. New **Super admin**: the only role that sees People and Hiring. Admin access is the role alone (no school-email rule). Every current admin becomes a super admin once (`super_admin_v1`); trim it on People.
- **Hiring:** candidates apply from their portal account at `/teach` → **Teaching** (CV upload, status tracker, the full application as a portal form, withdraw). Admin → Hiring board/table with stages, interview schedule, recommendations, notes, files and answers.
- **Families → Next step:** each prospective family gets one next step with the button that does it (reply, propose a tour time, invite to interview, decide, review, resend the ClassReach link, follow up, move to Hiring). Replaces the Follow-up column and filter.
- **Applications:** an **Accepted** stage that emails the ClassReach sign-up link; CSV export.
- **Calendar:** gatherings and school days can repeat; editing or deleting one date of anything repeating asks This date / This and following / All dates. Location suggestions (places used before, then Google Places when `GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY` is set).
- **Forms:** autosave with an indicator, required answers marked and checked before submit, the typed name is the signature (no checkbox), Save draft returns to the list, print or save as PDF, example forms in the dev seed.
- **Messages:** a student's conversation shows who is in it; an admin reading one they don't teach in sees a notice and opens the reply box on purpose; compact list rows.
- **Inquiry form:** asks what they're interested in (enrolling, learning more, visiting, teaching, other), which sets the inquiry's type.
- **Finance** (behind the Finance role only): budget scenarios, family accounts and tuition plans, manual payments, QuickBooks CSV, a payment-provider pipeline (no merchant yet). No budget data is in the repo; it is imported separately.
- **ClassReach matching** staging page, sidebar area switcher, and many smaller UI fixes.

## What happens to the data

The first request after the deploy runs the schema in one transaction. If anything fails, nothing changes and the previous deploy (Netlify rollback) still works.

Added only: tables for finance, hiring (`teacher_applicants`, `teacher_applicant_notes`), ClassReach matching and repeating events (`event_series`), plus new columns (`events.series_id`, `events.detached_from`, `tour_slots.detached_from`, `teacher_applicants.family_id/files`, `form_assignments.teacher_applicant_id`). Nothing is dropped or renamed, and no rows are deleted.

One-time changes, each guarded by a `settings` key:

| Key | Change | Reversible without a backup? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `private_tours_v1` | One-family tour times made for a "different time" request become private `private_tour` times (unpublished). | Yes |
| `applications_accepted_v1` | The application stage check allows `accepted`. | Yes (wider check) |
| `super_admin_v1` | Every admin also gets `super_admin`. | Yes |

Code-only rollback: the previous build ignores the new role, stage, slot type and tables.

## New emails

| Email | Sent when | Asks first? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted (ClassReach sign-up link) | Staff accept an application, or press Resend the link | Yes |
| Teacher application received | A candidate applies from Teaching | Their own action |
| Teacher: full application / video lesson / not moving forward | Staff move a candidate to that stage | Yes |

Moving a teaching inquiry to Hiring doesn't email the person.
